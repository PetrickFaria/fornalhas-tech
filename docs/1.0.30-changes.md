# Fornalhas Tech 1.0.30

Base branch: `main`; base commit `bac7a1a208b16d1199b49c1cd0b00b26812a8c31` (1.0.29).

## Fire orientation

The 1.0.29 renderer built one north-facing quad and attempted to place it on the other sides using a manually signed Y rotation. That left its location/orientation dependent on a second rotation separate from the blockstate's baked facing variants; the quad also carried a normal/winding pair authored only for the north face. In 1.0.30 the single quad is selected by `FACING` and placed directly on the corresponding front plane (north/east/south/west), with an outward normal and front-face vertex winding for each. Exactly one front panel is drawn at a time. The redstone/particle state logic and model geometry are untouched.

## Larger fire frames

Replaced the five `fire_panel_0.png`..`fire_panel_4.png` overlays with distinct 64x32 RGBA pixel-art frames. They fill the entire panel's 2:1 internal bounds (x=4.75..11.25, y=2.5..5.75 model units) with irregular flame tongues, transparent gaps and high tongues near the window top. The existing dark panel remains visible through transparent pixels. The overlay remains gated by `MODE == 0 && LIT`; it is independent of ingredients and runs continuously over fuel handoff.

## Particle rate

Changed only the probability gate in `TechFurnaceBlock.animateTick` from 0.35 to 0.525 per animate-tick invocation, exactly 1.5x. Particle scale (0.55), velocity, emission region, technology color lookup, and `COOKING` requirement are unchanged. Redstone output remains unchanged at strength 15 during `COOKING`.

## Verification

The 1.0.30 validator checks corner model invariants, all five PNG dimensions/alpha and uniqueness, four direction-specific fire faces/normals, Base/LIT gating, particle probability ratio, COOKING-only particles/redstone, inventory comparator signal and unchanged fuel/light invariants. Minecraft visual verification is still needed for the four facing directions.
